Privacy Policy

I'm Drinking · Last updated: 6 November 2026

I'm Drinking is an iPhone app that locks the apps you choose for a set time, so you don't text, post or spend money you'll regret. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. The app is made by Caiden Bryant, an individual developer in Canada.

The short version

  • No account and no sign-up. We don't run a server, and we don't collect your name, email, contacts or messages.
  • What you lock and when stays on your iPhone. We never see it.
  • The free app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device information to show and measure those ads, as explained below.

What stays on your iPhone

The app uses Apple's Screen Time tools (Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity) to lock apps and websites. Your app choices, lock times, settings and the "no deleting me" switch are saved only on your iPhone. Apple's Screen Time tools don't show the app which apps you pick from Apple's own list, only private tokens. None of this information is sent to us, to Google or to anyone else, and it is never used for advertising.

Lock and unlock reminders are local notifications created on your iPhone. Face ID or your passcode is checked by iOS itself, and the app never sees your face data or your passcode.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads and, when you choose to end a lock early, a short video ad. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), but only if you tap "Allow" on Apple's tracking prompt
  • your IP address and approximate location based on it
  • device and app information, such as model, iOS version, language and app version
  • how you interact with ads, for example views and taps

If you don't allow tracking, ads still show, but they won't use your advertising identifier. You can change this any time in iPhone Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking.

If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before ads are personalised. You can change your choice any time with "ad privacy choices" on the app's home screen.

Google's use of this information is covered by Google's own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

I'm Drinking is meant for adults of legal drinking age and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Since we don't hold any personal information about you, there's nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Deleting the app removes everything it saved on your iPhone. For information Google holds, use Google's tools linked above. If you have questions about privacy, or about how this policy applies under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA) or the law where you live, contact us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
